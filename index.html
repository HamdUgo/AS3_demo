The scenario

A person with a communication and language disorder asks for a reminder to call the pharmacy on Thursday afternoon to refill a prescription. The assistant hears Tuesday.

What the repair does

  • Confirm before actingNowWhen it is unsure, it says what it understood in plain words and asks.
  • Offer its best guessesNowAfter a no, it offers its two closest readings. Tap one or say its number.
  • Fix only the wrong partNowOne detail changes. Everything else is kept.
  • Undo and repeat, alwaysNowBoth commands work at every step. Repeat speaks more slowly.

The uncertainty cue

Sound
Two rising notes, played just before the word it doubts.
Touch
Two short buzzes on phones that vibrate.
Sight
A wavy underline and a Not sure tag on that word only.

Ways to answer

Speak with the microphone, tap an answer, or type. Speech input needs Chrome or Edge and microphone permission. Tapping and typing work everywhere.
